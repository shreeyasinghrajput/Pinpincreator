# Privacy Policy for PinCreator Analytics

Last updated: November 20, 2026

R Shreeya Singh (the "Developer") operates PinCreator Analytics (the "App"). We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information from users of our App.

## 1. Information We Collect
Because this is an analytics dashboard, we may request permission to connect to your Pinterest account using OAuth. If you choose to connect your Pinterest account, we will access:
- Your Pin and Board data (to display on your personal dashboard)
- Account analytics (impression, click, and save counts to show you progress graph metrics)

## 2. How We Use Information
We access this data solely to populate your analytics dashboard. We do NOT store, upload, or transfer any of your Pinterest data to external servers. Your data stays only in your local web environment and backend.

## 3. Data Sharing & Privacy
- We do not share, sell, or rent your private account information or metrics with third-party companies.
- We do not run ads on this App or use tracking cookies.
